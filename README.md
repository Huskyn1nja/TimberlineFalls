If you would like to create custom content of your own for Timberline, I will allow it with the following notes / Caveats:
There are future plans for many areas. Do not be surprised if I take over a spot you liked for a house
You may not move or change existing NPC homes locations. You are welcome to redo interiors but I ask you verify Major changes with me.
You may have your NPCs interact with timberline NPCs. However you must keep the Timberline residents true to who they are. (Example. NO, Bernard did NOT decide your NPC is great and is his best friend and is the only person he isn't mean to). This applies to mods that add dialogue or revise NPCs. 
No explicit or derogatory NPCs are to be part of the town. They can be mean, unlikable, etc. but you can do that without racism, sexism, etc
